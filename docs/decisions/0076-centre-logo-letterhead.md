# 0076 — Logo in the middle: a letterhead with Arabic on the right and English on the left

## Decision
- On A4 documents printed in **both languages** with the logo position **"middle"**, the header is a letterhead in three columns:
  - the Arabic company details on the right, in this order: the company name alone (no label, so it stands out), then labelled address, phone, email (phone and email when shown), then the TRN;
  - the logo in the middle;
  - the English details on the left.
- In one language only, "middle" keeps the logo above the details, centred.
- With the logo beside (or opposite) in both languages, the earlier block stays, without labels: Arabic name, English name, Arabic address, English address, the TRN line with the Arabic label on the right and "TRN:" on the left ("TRN: 100… :الرقم الضريبي", also for the customer's TRN), then phone · email.
- The same labelled lines and order (the name without a label, then العنوان / Address, رقم الهاتف / Phone, البريد / Email, الرقم الضريبي / TRN) are used with the logo in the middle and in one-language documents; Arabic lines carry Arabic labels, English lines English labels.
- The locked tax fields (seller address and TRN on tax documents) print in both columns as before.

## Why
The owner's sample letterhead puts the logo in the middle with each language on its own side.
