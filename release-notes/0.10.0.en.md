Go-live tools: the automated rehearsal, the hardware check, the reconciliation report, and the export for go-live day.

- «Start rehearsal» in System health (owner): the server plays a whole shop day by itself on a separate throwaway database, deletes it at the end, and says green or red. Your real data is never touched.
- «Hardware check» in System health: about 5 minutes on the POS PC (a test receipt, the drawer, a test label, and scanning the label's barcode), and an A4 test page on the laptop.
- System health says «Ready for go-live» once the rehearsal is green and the POS PC's check is all OK.
- The new reconciliation report (Reports, owner and accountant): 7 steps, green or red (stock, cash, invoices, VAT, accounts, audit log, backup), printed as a PDF for the accountant.
- 7 new checks of the books run every night, after every server start and in every restore test.
- A speed line in System health: how long searches and sales take.
- Every thermal receipt is also saved as a PDF on the PC by itself (Public Documents › ARTEPOOL › Receipts), even when the printer is off.
- «Export Excel» for items and suppliers, like customers, in the import's own columns: the file goes straight back in on go-live day.
- Searching items and scanning barcodes are 4–5 times faster with many items.
- Fixed: selling a kit at the POS gave an error.
- Fixed: when two tills and the office saved at the same moment, one of them could get "busy, try again".
- Fixed: the customers export had the walk-in customer without a phone, so the import refused it.
