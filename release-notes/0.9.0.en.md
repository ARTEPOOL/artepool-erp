Updates are much smaller, and every PC shows the install steps.

- A server PC update is now about 10 MB instead of about 270 MB; office PCs and the laptop about 6 MB.
- While the server PC installs, every PC shows "Updating ARTEPOOL" with the steps, and at the end whether the new version is running.
- System health → Last update shows how long each step took, and the reason if one failed.
- With a shift open it says "Update ready — will install after the shift is closed". Press "Install after shift close" and the update starts by itself once the shift is closed, without waiting for the night.
- The laptop and the office PCs update by themselves, only while the app is closed. Below 20% battery they wait for the charger.
- If WebView2 is missing or broken on a PC, a clear message appears and the full setup installs by itself.
- This update itself comes the old way, once; the ones after it are small.
