Connecting Google Drive now works, and the program tells you if Google stops the access.

- This update carries the program's registration at Google, so "Connect Google Drive" works on the server PC.
- The program asks Google at every upload, and every 6 hours in between.
- If Google ended the saved access, a red line appears in Backup and on the dashboard, with a "Connect Google Drive again" button.
- If Google refused the program's registration itself, the program says to call support.
- No internet is not a refusal: it still shows as a late off-site copy, as before.
