Protection from power cuts, the last invoice on the POS, and cancelled invoices marked.

- A sale the program confirmed stays saved even if the power goes at that moment. If the power goes before a sale is finished, there is no half invoice: no number is lost and no stock or cash moves.
- After every start of the server (after a power cut too), the program checks the books, the stock and the numbering by itself once.
- Settings → System health → Server PC has a new line: **Protection from power cuts**. If something is not right, you get an alert.
- The POS always shows the shift's **last invoice** (number, time, total) with a **Reprint** button. After a power cut, the cashier sees from it whether the last sale went through before ringing it again.
- A cancelled invoice now says **Cancelled** with its credit note number, in the invoices list and on its page, and appears under the "Cancelled" filter.
- Words between two stars in the release notes now show in bold instead of the stars.
