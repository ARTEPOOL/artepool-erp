A payment settles the invoice it is for, and every invoice shows one clear status.

- "Receive payment" on an invoice now pays that invoice. The amount still due is filled in, and before saving it says "Settles INV-… with AED …".
- A payment always settles open invoices first (the oldest first). Only the money left over becomes an advance payment invoice, and before it is made the screen shows its amount and VAT and asks you to confirm.
- "Choose invoices" with no amount typed now shows a message, never an advance by surprise.
- Keeping a payment as an advance while the customer still owes (such as a deposit for a new project) needs a special permission and a written reason, and is recorded.
- Each invoice shows one status: unpaid, partly paid or paid, with "Overdue" after its due date, "Partially credited", or "Cancelled" with the credit note's number.
- Invoices has tabs: Invoices / Credit notes / Debit notes, and an "Overdue" filter.
- The invoice page shows its history: issued, each receipt with its payment method, each credit note and how the money went back.
