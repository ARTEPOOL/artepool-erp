Choose a customer and see their balance at once.

- A coloured box under the customer on the A4 invoice, the receipt and the POS: green nothing due, yellow within the limit, orange late invoices or near the limit, red over the limit.
- The owner and the accountant see the amounts: outstanding, overdue, credit limit, available, and advance payments on their own line. Other users see only the colour and the words.
- The customers list has an "Outstanding" column and a "Has overdue invoices" filter.
- The customer page shows the open invoices with their age, the oldest unpaid date and the net position.
- A4 invoices can print an "Account summary" (previous balance, this invoice, paid now, total due), saved with the invoice so a reprint is the same. On for credit customers; change it in Settings → Print designer.
